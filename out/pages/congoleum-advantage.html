AirStep Advantage

Life is messy, but AirStep by Congoleum is easy to maintain and live with so you can get back to enjoying life’s moments. AirStep Advantage is our most fashion-forward, luxurious product.

  • Warmest and most cushioned choice underfoot
  • Extra thick wear layer resists rips, tears and gouging
  • UltraTec® backing offers installation options and masks subfloor imperfections
  • Dimensional printing creates extremely realistic and upscale visuals
  • Lifetime residential warranty
