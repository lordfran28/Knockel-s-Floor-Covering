Coordinating Trims and Moldings

Southwind moldings are designed to complement Southwind flooring with a beautiful, finished look that conceals edges and transitions. All Southwind moldings are tough and durable, scratch resistant, and easy to install.

  • Flush StairnoseSmooth edge for stairs

  • Overlap StairnoseCovers stair edge overlap
