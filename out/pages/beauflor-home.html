Blacktex® HD

Beautiful designs with embossed textures, vivid colors, and enhanced durability are combined to create a luxurious, waterproof flooring experience that looks great and is easy to maintain.

An innovative textile backing that enhances comfort underfoot, thermal insulation, and sound absorbency makes Beauflor luxury vinyl flooring a great choice for all types of lifestyles. 

Learn More  View Spec Sheet

Craftech

Easy to love, easy to install, easy to live with. Craftech luxury vinyl flooring, made in the USA, looks great and features outstanding durability. Designed for loose-lay or glue-down installation, Craftech offers impressive value with on-trend colors and designs.

Craftech luxury vinyl rolls are easy to clean and waterproof, making Craftech the ideal solution for high-traffic areas in your home.

Learn More  View Spec Sheet

Metro+

The Metro+ Collection is inspired by urban living and is the perfect solution for the multi-family segment.  Metro+ has been updated with new designs and colors, based on the latest trends in interiors, and is designed for stress-free living.

Metro+ boasts outstanding performance: it withstands spills, scuffs, and even scratches from family pets.  It is easy to install, easy to maintain, waterproof, durable and affordable.

Learn More  View Spec Sheet

Reflect+

Reflect+ Collection has been updated with new designs and colors that are perfect for transitional and modern interiors. There are 15 stunning designs for a variety of tastes and aesthetics.

Enjoy stress-free living and the beauty of stylish floors that are waterproof, durable, easy to install, and ready to transform any space. Make every day a relaxing day with the effortless elegance of Reflect+.

Learn More  View Spec Sheet

Omega+

The Omega+ collection showcases a range of wood and tile designs. The wood designs come in trendy colors like whitewash and gray, as well as classic honey brown and espresso tones. The tile designs feature mixed width and 12” x 12” sized tile visuals in an organic, earthy color palette.

Omega+ also features several designs that mix plank and tile visuals together to create unique hybrid looks.

Learn More  View Spec Sheet
