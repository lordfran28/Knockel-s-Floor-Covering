Peace of Mind with FloorScore®

We care about your wellbeing. That’s why every year we undergo stringent testing to verify that our floors are completely safe for your spaces.

What is FloorScore® Certification? + –

FloorScore® is an independent third-party certification program that requires rigorous testing to certify hard surface flooring and flooring adhesive products. Using a carefully established set of criteria, VOC and emission levels are tested for each product. These specially-formulated guidelines test for chemicals such as formaldehyde, acetaldehyde, and other organic chemicals to ensure the safety of each and every product.

FloorScore® is the most recognized indoor air quality (IAQ) standard that exists for hard surface flooring materials, adhesives, and underlayments. This program was developed by the Resilient Floor Covering Institute (RFCI) and Scientific Certification Systems (SCS).

What does this mean for me? + –

The long-term safety and happiness of our customers is of the utmost importance to the Happy Feet® team. That’s why we test and recertify our products year after year.

The FloorScore® seal verifies that our floors, adhesives, and underlayments meet strict guidelines and contribute to healthy indoor air quality levels in your spaces. That means when you purchase Happy Feet® products, you can breathe easy knowing they’re safe.

We believe happiness is built from the ground up, and we look forward to crafting your next space together.
