Frequently Asked Questions

Karndean Designflooring is a brand of luxury vinyl flooring known for replicating the look and feel of natural materials like wood and stone, offering durability, easy maintenance, and a wide range of design options.

Gluedown flooring is affixed to the subfloor with adhesive. Individual planks and tiles can be cut and laid in patterns, allowing you to create stylish custom looks or zoning that sets an area apart. The possibilities are endless. View the benefits of gluedown flooring.

Rigid core is an easy-to-install “floating floor,” which means adhesive is not required. View the benefits of rigid core.
