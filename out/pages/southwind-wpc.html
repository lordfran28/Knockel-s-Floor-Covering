WPC

Home / WPC

At Southwind, our Wood Plastic Composite (WPC) flooring embodies comfort and quality. With its lightweight expanded polymer core, it offers a cushioned feel underfoot, while delivering exceptional sound absorption. The top layer of luxury vinyl ensures rich, authentic textures, so whether you choose a hardwood or slate look, your floors will have the stunning, natural finish you expect — The Southwind Way.
