PureGrain Renew

Engineered to be as sustainable as it is beautiful

Manufactured from renewable wood resources and PVC-free, PureGrain Renew is our most sustainable flooring option. Crafted from the heart of nature, every step is a gentle reminder of the world’s beauty.

Key Features & Benefits

PVC Free

No off-gassing of potentially dangerous chemicals

Eco Friendly

The most sustainable flooring made from 90% renewable wood resources

Scratch Resistant

Stands up to everyday life while maintaining its original beauty

Pet Friendly

Worry-free flooring for your furry companions

Waterproof

Spills and stains wipe away, leaving nothing behind

How we make PureGrain Renew

1 — Melamine with Aluminum Oxide
The melamine resin makes the paper incredibly durable, resistant to scratches, stains, and moisture.

2 — Decor Layer
This layer gives the floor its design and appearance, mimicking the look of natural materials like wood.

3 — High-Density HDF Core layer
High density HDF core, made from recycled wood materials like sawdust and wood pulp.

4 — Attached Underlayment Layer
The attached pad provides both sound reduction and enhanced comfort properties on select PureGrain Renew styles.
