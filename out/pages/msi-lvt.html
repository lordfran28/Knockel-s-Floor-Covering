      RIGID CORE SERIES

      Rigid core flooring is a durable type of vinyl plank with a solid core made from materials like SPC or WPC. It is waterproof, dent-resistant, and installs easily with a click-lock system over most subfloors. This makes it ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and high-traffic areas. Plus, it offers the authentic look of hardwood or stone at a fraction of the cost with virtually no maintenance required.

      GLUE DOWN SERIES

      Glue down flooring is glued directly to the subfloor, providing a secure and stable surface. It allows for precise patterns and handles heavy traffic or rolling loads well.

      LOOSE LAY SERIES

      Loose lay flooring consists of vinyl planks or tiles that simply lay flat on the subfloor without glue or locking. Its weight and friction backing hold it in place, while allowing easy removal or replacement.

      LEARN MORE ABOUT LUXURY VINYL FLOORING?

      Luxury Vinyl Flooring Installation
      1. Understanding the Floor Plans - Laying out a floor plan for luxury vinyl flooring is important, considering factors like room size, shape, vinyl type, and desired patterns. Depending on your preferences, you can create a grid pattern, install tiles alternately, or design something custom. Generally, starting from a corner is advisable
      2. Follow the Installation methods - Installing luxury vinyl flooring is a straightforward process that involves using an underlayment for support, placing planks or tiles, and securing them. You can use a glue-down method for standard vinyl or a floating click-lock method for luxury vinyl tiles and planks. To ensure proper installation, measure the room, acquire sufficient material, prepare the clean, level, dry subfloor, add underlayment for cushioning and soundproofing, and start installing from a corner, ensuring a secure water-resistant seal.
      3. Consider Installation Costs - When exploring flooring options, luxury vinyl flooring is often more cost-effective than hardwood or natural stone. The combined price for luxury vinyl flooring, including its installation, is usually $1.99 to $9.00 per square foot. The Dryback Gluedown type is flexible, while the Rigid Core type has a sturdy core and an intuitive locking design. The Dryback Series installation costs are around $1.99-$5.00 per square foot, and the Rigid Core Series is priced between $2.29-$9.00 per square foot once installed. As with any significant investment, it's a good idea to consult with a flooring expert to get a detailed and accurate quote before starting the installation.
      4. Focus on Professional Installation - When it comes to installing luxury vinyl flooring, you have several installation options. You can choose to hire a professional installer, or you can opt for a do-it-yourself approach. Professional installation may be the best choice if you don't have experience with this type of flooring. A professional installer will ensure that the job is done correctly and efficiently, avoiding potential issues.

      Luxury Vinyl Flooring Durability

      With its longevity and easy maintenance, Everlife® Luxury Vinyl Flooring is a preferred choice for various environments. Luxury vinyl planks and tiles have a core layer made of a fortified blend of materials, enhancing the flooring's strength and resilience. It comes equipped with an MSI exclusive CrystaLuxTM protective layer, ensuring durability and safeguarding against daily wear and scratches. The added thickness of luxury vinyl increases its hardiness, and its resistance to scratches makes it ideal for homes with pets or children.

      Scratch Resistance

      Luxury vinyl's resilience, durability, and scratch resistance stem from its protective wear layer. Ideal for households with pets or children, it's easily cleaned with a damp cloth. Most options include a manufacturer’s warranty, providing peace of mind against future issues.

      Water Resistance

      Luxury vinyl flooring provides scratch resistance and excellent waterproof protection, ideal for spill-prone areas like bathrooms and kitchens. Enhanced by a waterproof finish, it suits humid climates and water-adjacent homes. Certain planks offer a waterproof warranty, further safeguarding against water damage and ensuring prolonged spill protection.

      Noise Protection

      Luxury vinyl flooring is also known for its noise reduction capabilities. It's thick construction and tightly-bonded layers help to absorb sound, making it an ideal choice for noisy households. It's also suitable for multi-story homes, as its sound dampening qualities can prevent sound from traveling between floors.

      Abrasion Resistance

      Luxury vinyl flooring is highly resistant to abrasion, making it a great choice for high-traffic areas like hallways and living rooms. Its wear layer helps to protect the core from scratches, dents, and other damage caused by daily foot traffic. This makes it an ideal choice for households with pets or children who tend to be rougher on floors than adults.

      Dent Resistance

      Luxury vinyl flooring is also highly resistant to dents and indentations, thanks to its wear layer and stone based core. These fortified layers help to protect the core from damage caused by everyday household activity.

      FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

      How to Choose the Right Luxury Vinyl Tile Flooring?

      When choosing the best luxury vinyl tile flooring, consider factors such as your budget, the size of the space you’re covering, and the level of wear and tear it will be subject to. You should also look at style options, as there are many different designs available. It is important to choose a product with good reviews and warranties for long-term durability.

      Is Luxury Vinyl Flooring Suitable for High-Traffic and Commercial Spaces?

      Luxury vinyl flooring excels in high-traffic and commercial spaces due to its durability against scratches, dents, fading, and wear. Its low-maintenance nature ensures prolonged endurance, making it an enduring choice.

      What Are the Benefits of Luxury Vinyl Flooring?
      • Durability: Luxury vinyl flooring boasts exceptional durability, ensuring years of use with proper upkeep.
      • Moisture Resistance: Its high moisture resistance deems it fitting for bathrooms and kitchens.
      • Cost-Effective: Among the most budget-friendly options in the market.
      • Ease of Installation: Simple installation process contributes to its appeal.
      • Low Maintenance:Requires minimal maintenance, preferred for long-lasting and easy-care floors.
