Protect the moments that make a house a home with LifeProtect®, part of Paramount Flooring’s TimbrCORE Collections. Proudly made in the USA, this waterproof wood-core flooring combines beautiful, authentic visuals with exceptional resistance to scratches, stains, fading, and everyday wear. An attached pad adds comfort and helps soften sound, while its PVC-free, GREENGUARD Gold certified construction supports a healthier home. Designed for kids, pets, spills, and busy lifestyles, LifeProtect makes living beautifully feel effortless.
