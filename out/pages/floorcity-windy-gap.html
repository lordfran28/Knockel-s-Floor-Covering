Description

Engineered Floors PureGrain Renew Wood Tech offers rich oak-inspired styles with laminate durability and sustainable design—perfect for everyday family life.

  • Eco-Friendly & PVC-Free: Made from 90% renewable wood resources with no PVC, delivering a more sustainable flooring choice and healthier indoor air quality.
  • Waterproof Design: Engineered to resist spills and stains for easy, worry-free cleanup.
  • Durable Construction: Built with a melamine-finished decorative layer, high-density HDF core, and lasting performance under daily wear.
  • Layered Comfort: Includes an attached underlayment that adds sound absorption and cushioned feel underfoot.
  • 10 mm Thickness: Solid 10 mm plank offers a sturdy base and feels comparable to premium laminate flooring.

Combining sustainability, comfort, and durable oak styling, PureGrain Renew Wood Tech brings responsible innovation and long-lasting quality to your flooring projects.
